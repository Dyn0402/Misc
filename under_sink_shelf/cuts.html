Tablette en pin massif 40 × 200 cm, ép. 18 mm (PPA18400). Toutes les cotes en millimètres, vue de dessus. Cut sheet to show at the Castorama cutting desk. All sizes in mm, seen from above. The board is 400 wide; the long edges are called A (top) and B (bottom).

3. Une étagère finie · one finished shelf (both are the same)

Contrôle : poser les deux étagères l'une sur l'autre, elles doivent coïncider. Le devant fait 100–102 mm. Check: lay one shelf on the other, they should match. The front edge comes out at 100 to 102 mm.

4. Si le service refuse la coupe en biais · if they won't do the diagonal

  • Have them make cuts 1 and 2 (straight crosscuts, the easy ones for a panel saw) and do 3 to 5 at home with the hand saw.
  • At home: on the 618 piece, mark 63 on edge A and 555 on edge B from the same end, draw the line, clamp a straight batten along it as a guide and saw on the line (the line is the shared edge: split it). Then cuts 4 and 5 with a square.
  • If they trim a factory end first, it comes off the last piece, which gets recut later anyway.
  • Castorama does not publish a price per cut online (worktops are 5 € per cut); ask at the desk.

5. Later: the oak for the folding table · chêne 18 mm, pour plus tard

Only the straight cuts, which do not depend on the unanswered fridge-hinge question. The angled ends of the top, its rounded corners and the corbel diagonal are cut at home once the hinge side is confirmed. The long rip cuts are the ones worth having done here: a 2 m rip in oak by hand is the hardest cut in either project.
