The totals count one pack of each line, as listed. Shipping is not included: Castorama marketplace sellers and Amazon each show their own delivery terms, and the Amazon pages were priced for a Paris 75001 address, not Palaiseau. Stock at the Palaiseau store was not checked.

What the comparison found

  • Timber: Castorama only. I found no like-for-like 18 mm × 40 × 200 cm pine board or 27 × 27 × 2.4 m pine batten on Amazon. The results were craft strips and cutting boards.
  • Brackets: Amazon is cheaper and better. A pack of 22 stainless 40 × 40 mm brackets with 88 screws is €9.75. Castorama's pack of 25 is galvanised, €13.90, and has no screws. You need 9. Check the screw length in the Amazon listing before ordering: the screws that go through a bracket into an 18 mm shelf must be 16 mm or shorter. Otherwise add the 3.5 × 16 mm stainless screws from Castorama.
  • Tools and consumables: Castorama is cheaper. Saw (€7.99 against €16.70 for the Stanley), clamps, sandpaper and a brush all cost less there.
  • The 4 × 40 mm stainless screws are a tie. €19.90 for 200 Pozidriv at Castorama, €20.42 for 200 Torx at Amazon. You need about 12 of them, so most of the pack is spare either way.
  • Linseed oil: Amazon does not list the Phébus 1 L. The closest 1 L is a baked linseed oil at €9.30 with 8 reviews.

Notes on the picks

  • The brackets go on the three post-to-shelf joints of each shelf. 40 × 40 × 20 mm is enough, because the posts are 27 mm wide.
  • The batten page lists a bevelled edge, so the posts will have slightly eased edges.
  • For the damp cabinet the glue should be water-resistant (D3). The Pattex Power pick is rated "water resistant", with no D-class on the listing. Sader Pro D3 (100 g, €9.89) is the one that states D3.
  • A pilot drill bit (2.5 mm) and a countersink are not on the list. Add them if you do not have them.
