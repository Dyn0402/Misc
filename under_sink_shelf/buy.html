Buying with the folding table in mind

The table is not bought today, only looked at. These shelf picks are chosen so they also serve it:

  • Screws: the 200-pack of 4 × 40 A2 (allée 114). 24 go into the shelf and risers, 12 into the table's rail, and the rest are spares.
  • Saw: Magnusson 500 mm, 11 teeth per inch, coupe fine (€16.90, allée 119, 12 in stock). The fine teeth leave a clean edge in pine and oak, and 500 mm is long enough for the oak later. If it's not on the shelf, in order of preference (all allée 119):
    • Magnusson 450 mm, 11 TPI, €11.50: the same teeth, a bit shorter. Fine for the shelf and the battens.
    • Magnusson 500 mm, 9 TPI "coupe universelle", €18.90: faster, slightly rougher edge.
    • Stanley STHT20350-1, 500 mm, 8 TPI, €18.90 (only 1 in stock).
    • Budget: 400 mm, 7 TPI, €5.19. Rough but works for the shelf; sand the cut ends.
    Avoid saws labelled plaque de plâtre / cloison sèche (drywall) or métaux (metal). Look for "bois" and 8 to 11 dents par pouce (TPI). A Magnusson mitre box with its own saw (boîte à onglet et scie, €11.90, allée 72, next to the battens) is a good extra for square batten cuts.
  • Glue and clamps: one 225 g Pattex bottle and the pair of 300 mm clamps cover gluing the table's rail as well.
  • Optional, for both: a carpenter's square (équerre de menuisier) to mark every cut,.
  • Looking at the oak: the 60 × 200 × 18 mm oak board is in allée 136 (€66.90, 9 in stock). Hold it next to a photo of your counter, and check the boards for twist and cracks. The cut plan for it is on the Découpe tab for when you buy it.

What the Castorama / Amazon comparison found (Oct)

  • Castorama versus Amazon overall: the timber is only at Castorama, so a Castorama trip is needed either way. Buying everything else on Amazon too costs more (about €122 against €111), mostly because of clamps, brush and sandpaper. The cheapest mix is Castorama for everything except the brackets.
  • Screws: the 200-pack. You need 24 of the 4 × 40 mm for the shelf and risers, plus 12 for the folding table's rail. The 200-pack (€19.90, 5 in stock at Les Ulis, allée 114) covers both with spares; two packs of 20 (€11.78) would cover only the shelf. On Amazon, 200 A2 Torx 4 × 40 cost €14.90.
  • Timber: Castorama only. I found no like-for-like 18 mm × 40 × 200 cm pine board or 27 × 27 × 2.4 m pine batten on Amazon. The results were craft strips and cutting boards.
  • Brackets: Castorama, in store. Diall stainless chair brackets, 15 mm wide, 40 × 40 mm, 4 per pack (€7.59, 5 packs in stock, allée 113): three packs give 12, for the 9 kitchen joints and 3 spare. Stainless suits the damp cabinet and 15 mm is narrower than the 27 mm posts. They come without screws. The galvanised pack of 25 (€13.90) is not stocked at Les Ulis. On Amazon, VFANDV's 20 stainless brackets with screws are €7.79 but need waiting for delivery.
  • Tools and consumables: Castorama is cheaper. Clamps and the brush cost less there. Saws cost about the same on Amazon, but the Castorama one can be picked up today.
  • Linseed oil: Amazon does not list the Phébus 1 L. The closest 1 L is a baked linseed oil at €9.30 with 8 reviews.

Notes on the picks

  • The brackets go on the three post-to-shelf joints of each shelf. The 15 mm Diall brackets are narrower than the 27 mm posts. Pre-drill 2 mm for the short screws, and keep the shelf-side screws at 16 mm or shorter so they cannot come through the 18 mm shelf.
  • The batten page lists a bevelled edge, so the posts will have slightly eased edges.
  • For the damp cabinet the glue should be water-resistant (D3). The Pattex Power pick is rated "water resistant", with no D-class on the listing. Sader Pro D3 (100 g, €9.89) is the one that states D3.
  • A pilot drill bit (2.5 mm) and a countersink are not on the list. Add them if you do not have them.
  • The saw is a straight hand saw rather than the coping saw on earlier versions of this list: every cut left is straight.
  • Jar riser for the kitchen top closet: a top of up to 600 × 400 mm on four 20 mm dowel legs, 162 mm long, 18 cm tall overall. The legs come from your two 1 m dowels, so it costs nothing extra. The top fits on the same board only if the two kitchen shelves are cut nested (turned 180° to each other, see the cut plan on the Design tab), which brings them from 860 mm of board down to 618 mm. The shop leaves the end of the board (about 624 mm) as this piece; cut it to the closet's size yourself. Fix each leg with one 4 × 40 screw down through the top into a 2.5 mm pilot hole in the centre of the dowel end, plus glue. After all three pieces, about 175 mm of batten and the dowel offcuts are left.
  • Closet riser: a 750 × 400 mm top (the closet is about 76 cm wide with obstacles) on four 27 × 27 mm legs, 182 mm long, 200 mm tall overall. Fix each leg with two 4 × 40 screws down through the top. Brackets and glue are optional on the riser: its legs are short and only carry weight straight down. The cut plan is in closet_riser.py.
