Folding kitchen table — plan v2

Design Cost & buy list Folding table

A wall-hung bar table for the kitchen, on the wall the fridge's side stands against. Two folding brackets screw to the brick. The table pulls out to 120 cm, for a 70 cm stool, and folds down against the wall, where its finished face looks out like an oak panel. The fridge-side edge is cut at an angle so the table is longest at the wall, and it stays at least cm clear of the fridge door, out or folded. All dimensions in cm unless stated.

Do we need three supports? No, two. The brackets are what hold the table, so the question is the load on each one. If a 60 kg person leans on the outer edge right above one bracket, that is 0.6 kN. The brace carries about 0.9 kN along its length and the pivot is pulled about 0.7 kN out of the wall. A 500 mm folding bracket rated for 100 kg takes about 1 kN, and a normal load is far smaller. Between the brackets, a 28 mm oak top spans 79 cm. A 40 kg load in the middle bends it by about half a millimetre. The third bracket (and the rail) would only add cost and holes in the brick. If you plan to sit on the table or put heavy equipment on it, add a third in the middle instead of relying on the rating.

The clearance numbers are produced by folding_table/check_clearance.mjs, which sweeps the door from 0 to 120° against the table at five fold angles, the bracket plates and the bins.

5. Fixing to a brick wall

  1. Find out which brick it is. French brick walls are often hollow terracotta (brique creuse) under 1–2 cm of plaster. Drill one 6 mm test hole at the height of the pivot. Red dust and then a steady resistance means solid brick (brique pleine). Dust that stops, then a sudden void, means hollow brick.
  2. Hollow brick: chemical anchors with a mesh sleeve (scellement chimique avec tamis): M8 or M10 threaded rod, resin cartridge and 16 mm sleeve, as sold for hollow brick by fischer, Spit and Sika. A plug alone will not hold a lever load in hollow brick.
  3. Solid brick: frame anchors (chevilles à cadre) 10 × 100 mm or longer, or the same resin without the sleeve. Keep each hole at least 5 cm from a brick edge and out of the mortar joints where you can.
  4. Pattern: 4 fixings per bracket plate, 8 in all: two at the top, near the pivot, which take the pull-out, and two at the bottom, near the brace pivot. Mark the holes on the plate, level it, then drill.
  5. Check there is nothing in the wall (cables, pipes) with a detector before drilling. The fridge and the sink wall may share services.
  6. Plaster is soft. Choose anchors long enough to pass the 1–2 cm of render and sit in the brick, and do not tighten them so far that the plaster crumbles.

6. Wood and finish

Wood. Solid oak (chêne massif) edge-glued panel, 28 mm, with the grain running along the wall. Oak is hard enough for a table you will lean on, takes stain and oil evenly, and matches most wooden worktops. I do not know what your counters are made of. Take a stained offcut to the shop and hold it against the counter before you buy the panel. You need a piece about 140 × 60 cm; the usual 200 × 60 cm panel is enough, with an offcut left for tests.

Can you stain it and keep it easy to clean? Yes. Two routes that give a finished, wipe-clean surface:

RouteWhat you doGoodWatch out
Hardwax oil with colour
closest to a wood worktop		 Sand to P150, one coat of tinted hardwax oil (Osmo Polyx-Oil tints, or Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2C, which colours and seals in one coat). Wipe off the excess. Colour and protection in one step. Food-safe when cured. Scratches and rings can be spot-repaired. Full hardness takes about a week. It needs a re-oil every year or two, and it is less proof against a spilled pan of wine than a varnish.
Stain, then water-based polyurethane
hardest-wearing		 Sand to P150, one coat of water-based stain, then 3 coats of water-based polyurethane varnish rated for worktops (vernis polyuréthane phase aqueuse, satin), light sanding with P240 between coats. A sealed film: the easiest to wipe, and water, coffee and oil sit on top. Looks less like bare wood. A deep scratch cannot be spot-repaired: the whole top is re-sanded.
  • Test both on an offcut cut from the same panel, and look at it next to your counter in daylight.
  • Finish every face, the edges and the underside with the same number of coats, or the top will cup.
  • Finish the table before it goes on the wall. Folded down, its top face is the one you see from the room, so that is the face to do most carefully.
  • Round the outer edges with a 3 mm radius (a few passes of sandpaper on a block). It chips less and wipes cleaner than a sharp arris.
  • Clear the table before folding it down. Anything left on it slides off.

  1. Drill test, then choose the anchors.
  2. Cut the shape from the panel (check_clearance.mjs prints the corner points) and round the two outer corners to a 5 cm radius.
  3. Sand, stain, and finish. Let it cure for a week.
  4. Mark the bracket plates 72 and 151 cm from the fridge front, with the pivot 117 cm up. Check they are level and parallel, then anchor them.
  5. Screw the table to the arms, with its back edge at the wall.
  6. Fold it down and open the fridge door fully to check the clearance on the real wall, then pull it out and do the same.

8. Open questions

  • Door hinge side. You said the hinge is on the right. In the photo the handle grooves look to be on the right edge, which would mean a left hinge. If the hinge is on the wall side, the whole shape changes (the table would be widest away from the wall). Please check.
  • Wall behind the fridge. I drew it 15 cm thick, flush with the fridge back, with a 90 cm wide, 205 cm high doorway starting 11 cm from the fridge's right side. These are guesses from the photo. Please measure the doorway start and width.
  • Bin positions. I assumed the recycle bin starts 60 cm from the fridge front and the trash bin follows it. At that position the recycle bin is only about 4 cm from the swinging freezer door, so measure it.
  • Bracket brace. Models differ in how the brace folds. The animation shows a knee-jointed stay, straight when the table is out. Pick a bracket whose folded length is 50 cm or less.
  • What species and finish are the counters?
  • Wall length to the left of the fridge: the table needs 172 cm from the fridge front, plus stool room.
