A wall-hung bar table for the kitchen, on the wall beside the fridge (the wall the fridge's side stands against). It is hinged to an oak rail on the brick, held up by three folding brackets, and folds flat against the wall. 5. Fixing to a brick wall

  1. Find out which brick it is. French brick walls are often hollow terracotta (brique creuse) under 1–2 cm of plaster. Drill one 6 mm test hole at the rail height. Red dust and then a steady resistance means solid brick (brique pleine). Dust that stops, then a sudden void, means hollow brick.
  2. Hollow brick: chemical anchors with a mesh sleeve (scellement chimique avec tamis): M8 or M10 threaded rod, resin cartridge and 16 mm sleeve, as sold for hollow brick by fischer, Spit and Sika. A plug alone will not hold a lever load in hollow brick.
  3. Solid brick: frame anchors (chevilles à cadre) 10 × 100 mm or longer, or the same resin without the sleeve. Keep each hole at least 5 cm from a brick edge and out of the mortar joints where you can.
  4. Pattern: 6 anchors through the rail (two rows of three, at the brackets' X positions), plus 2 per bracket plate at the brace pivot. Pre-drill the rail with counterbored holes and plug them with oak dowels cut from the offcut.
  5. Load: a 60 kg person leaning on the outer edge pulls about 0.9 kN on the top of each bracket pair and pushes about 1.2 kN along the brace. Three brackets rated for 100 kg each leave a wide margin. The anchors are the part to over-build.
  6. Check there is nothing in the wall (cables, pipes) with a detector before drilling. The fridge and the sink wall may share services.

6. Wood and finish

Wood. Solid oak (chêne massif) edge-glued panel, 28 mm, with the grain running along the wall. Oak is hard enough for a table you will lean on, takes stain and oil evenly, and matches most wooden worktops. I do not know what your counters are made of. Take a stained offcut to the shop and hold it against the counter before you buy the panel. A 200 × 60 cm panel is enough for the top (about 0.61 m²) and the offcut makes the rail if it is wide enough. Otherwise buy a 27 × 100 mm oak board for the rail.

Can you stain it and keep it easy to clean? Yes. Two routes that give a finished, wipe-clean surface:

RouteWhat you doGoodWatch out
Hardwax oil with colour
closest to a wood worktop		 Sand to P150, one coat of tinted hardwax oil (Osmo Polyx-Oil tints, or Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2C, which colours and seals in one coat). Wipe off the excess. Colour and protection in one step. Food-safe when cured. Scratches and rings can be spot-repaired. Full hardness takes about a week. It needs a re-oil every year or two, and it is less proof against a spilled pan of wine than a varnish.
Stain, then water-based polyurethane
hardest to stain		 Sand to P150, one coat of water-based stain, then 3 coats of water-based polyurethane varnish rated for worktops (vernis polyuréthane phase aqueuse, satin), light sanding with P240 between coats. A sealed film: the easiest to wipe, and water, coffee and oil sit on top. Looks less like bare wood. A deep scratch cannot be spot-repaired: the whole top is re-sanded.
  • Test both on an offcut cut from the same panel, and look at it next to your counter in daylight.
  • Finish every face, the edges and the underside with the same number of coats, or the top will cup. The edge faces and underside can take one coat less than the top for the oil route.
  • Finish the table before it goes on the wall. Finish the top face the most carefully: it ends up against the rail when folded, so any sticky finish should be fully cured before the first fold.
  • Round the outer edges with a 3 mm radius (a few passes of sandpaper on a block). It chips less and wipes cleaner than a sharp arris.

  2. Cut the trapezoid from the panel (outline from check_clearance.mjs, which prints the corner points) and round the two outer corners to a 5 cm radius.
  3. Sand, stain, and finish. Let it cure for a week.
  4. Fix the rail with its top edge 135 cm up and its fridge end 32 cm from the fridge front. Check it is level.
  5. Screw the hinge to the rail, then to the top face at the back edge. Hang the table level.
  6. Fit the brackets at 75, 118 and 161 cm, with the brace pivot at 72 cm up the wall.
  7. Fold it up and open the fridge door fully, to check the clearance on the real wall.
